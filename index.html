What actually makes a prompt work

Sky Prompt Designer is built around four instruments. Skip any of them and the model has to guess — which is where most disappointing answers come from.

role

Who is answering

A role narrows the model's assumptions about vocabulary, depth, and priorities before it writes a word.

context

What it can't guess

Facts, constraints, and background specific to your situation. Without it, the model fills gaps with plausible-sounding averages.

format

The shape of the answer

Naming the output shape — list, table, email — saves a rewrite. Models default to prose unless told otherwise.

constraints

The edges to stay inside

Length, tone limits, and things to avoid turn a generic answer into one that fits the one place it needs to go.
